FIRST TIME HERE?

PRACTICE FIGHT

Learn by playing. Emi fights next to you and shows you how to answer, attack, steal a turn and use your Ultimate, one step at a time. Nothing is timed. It takes about 3 minutes.

HOW TO PLAY

⚔ BATTLE BASICS

  • Each player takes turns. Every question shows on its own for 4 seconds first: read it and think of the answer before the choices appear.
  • ✓ Correct — your attack is powered up. Choose Strike, a Skill, your Ultimate or an Item.
  • Tap SKILLS to see your four skill cards: what each one does, its SP cost and its cooldown.
  • Stuck? Tap 💡 HINT on your question. A right answer after a hint is not powered up and adds only half as much Sync.
  • ✗ Wrong — you Guard automatically. No attacks or items.

📖 LEARN AS YOU FIGHT

  • Answer along: when it is someone else's turn, answer their question too. You find out if you were right when the question ends, and a right answer adds Sync for your team.
  • Back again: a question you get wrong comes back to you later, so you can get it right.
  • Write it down: after the battle, write short answers in full sentences about what you missed, then compare with the right answer.

⚡ STEAL THE TURN

  • When someone answers wrong, a quick steal window (about 6 seconds) opens.
  • Any other player can buzz in and answer the same question.
  • First correct stealer takes the turn — but ATTACKS ARE HALVED!
  • If nobody steals in time, the original player guards.

🔋 TEAM SYNC GAUGE

  • The Sync Gauge fills when your team answers correctly in a row. Both teammates' answers count.
  • The first right answer starts the streak. Every right answer after it fills the gauge, and more the longer the streak goes.
  • A wrong answer, or a missed steal, ends the streak. The gauge keeps what it has.
  • At 100%, unleash your ULTIMATE move (needs a correct answer too!). Every Ultimate breaks the battle stage a little more.

🎒 ITEMS

  • Each player starts every battle with 3 items. Use one instead of a move, after a correct answer:
  • Kaya Toast Set — restore 300 HP to one ally
  • Malt Dino Elixir — restore 150 SP to one ally
  • Phoenix Kueh — bring back a KO'd teammate at 50% HP
  • 🏪 In a class tournament, spend the coins you earn at Auntie's shop between rounds. She sells 9 items, from Durian Bombs to Study Notes.
