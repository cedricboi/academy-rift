FIELD GUIDE

Lead the advance.

Both standards begin at your Crystal Fortress. Optional branches offer movement shrines and monster concepts.

  1. Select a company. Tap their portrait or banner. Open Banners to choose from all ten characters while at the fortress.
  2. Choose a waypoint. Stone roads cost 1 MP; rough trails cost 2 MP. Turn on route costs to inspect the network. Tap another waypoint to extend the route; tap an earlier one to shorten it.
  3. Commit or hold. Unspent points stay in reserve for this round. You can revise orders before marching. Each new round starts with 4 MP, plus any shrine bonus earned by arriving in the previous round. Holding at a shrine does not earn another bonus.
  4. Begin the march. All ordered companies depart together. Start the next round when they arrive.
