# Crimson Host concept sheets — prompts

Two sheets, six creatures each, made to sit beside `monster-concepts.png`
("Creatures of the Moonveil"). Generate each prompt separately. Attach
`monster-concepts.png` and `moonveil-branches.png` as style references so the
pixel density, lighting and lettering match.

Save the results as:
- `public/total-war/art/crimson-host-1.png`
- `public/total-war/art/crimson-host-2.png`

Check list for every result before keeping it:
- Exactly six creatures, three across and two down, each on its own small terrain pedestal.
- Each creature's name is spelled exactly as given, in small light-gold serif capitals beneath it, and no other text except the title and subtitle.
- Crimson, black and rose-gold are the main colours, so the host reads as the enemy army against the teal and blue student side.
- No humans, no student characters, no blood, no gore, no weapons pointed at the viewer.
- Same pixel density and finish as `monster-concepts.png`.

## Sheet 1 — The Crimson Host: Rank and File

Create an exquisite professional monster concept-art presentation sheet for Academy Rift: Total War, a gothic woodland fantasy strategy game. Landscape 3:2 composition, 1536x1024. SIX distinct full-body creature designs arranged neatly in 3 columns by 2 rows on a dark midnight parchment background, each in its own generous space with a subtle crimson elemental halo and a small terrain pedestal of cracked red-veined stone. Polished, detailed pixel-art / high-end 2D JRPG sprite illustration matching the supplied Academy Rift "Creatures of the Moonveil" sheet exactly in pixel density, rim lighting and finish. These are the soldiers that pour out of the Crimson Fortress through the rift. They share one visual language: blackened iron armour with rose-gold trim, glowing crimson rift crystals set into armour and weapons, torn crimson banners and cloth. NO humans, NO existing branded creatures, no blood, no gore, no UI boxes. Title at top: 'THE CRIMSON HOST', small subtitle 'ACADEMY RIFT / TOTAL WAR — RANK AND FILE'. Each creature has ONLY its exact name in refined small light-gold serif lettering beneath it.

Top left RIFTBORN ORC VANGUARD: broad muscular orc foot soldier with grey-green skin, small tusks, heavy blackened iron pauldrons with rose-gold rims, a tall tower shield bearing a crimson crystal crest, a short heavy blade held low, stable wide stance, disciplined rather than savage.

Top middle ORC WARCHANTER: older orc shaman with a long braided beard, bone-and-iron headdress, robes of layered crimson cloth, carrying a war drum on his back and a staff topped with a pulsing red crystal, faint red sound-wave rings rising from the drum, a support unit that rallies others.

Top right EMBERFANG GOBLIN SAPPERS: a group of three small wiry goblins sharing one pedestal, oversized goggles, leather aprons, carrying a crate of glowing red crystal charges and a long fuse, mischievous and quick, clearly saboteurs who break bridges and walls.

Bottom left RIFT HOUND: lean wolf-like beast of black smoke and obsidian plates, crimson cracks of light along its body, three glowing eyes, ember sparks trailing from its paws, low hunting crouch, a fast scout that patrols roads.

Bottom middle CRIMSON GARGOYLE: winged stone sentinel carved from dark red granite, crouched on a broken battlement, crystal heart glowing in its chest, horns and folded bat-like wings, the guard that sits on the fortress walls and watchtowers.

Bottom right HOLLOW KNIGHT OF THE RIFT: an empty suit of tall gothic black plate armour with no body inside, crimson light glowing through the visor slit and joints, a long halberd, tattered crimson cape, elite defender of bridges and gates.

All six creatures must be visually distinct in silhouette and size, same pixel density and consistent production quality. Beautiful rim lighting, crisp silhouettes suitable for later game sprites. Deliver finished art, not a website mockup.

These are the heavy units and commanders of the Crimson Fortress, larger and more imposing than the rank and file. Shared visual language: blackened iron with rose-gold trim, glowing crimson rift crystals, torn crimson banners. NO humans, NO existing branded creatures, no blood, no gore, no UI boxes. Top middle EMBER IMP SWARM: a cloud of five tiny winged imps with red skin and stubby horns, each carrying a small glowing coal, swirling together in a spiral above a single pedestal, playful but troublesome, a harassing flying unit.
